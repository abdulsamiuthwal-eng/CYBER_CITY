AUGUST 12, 2050 • DISTRICT 4 TRANSIT AUTHORITY

NEW SKYWAY DRONE CORRIDOR OFFICIALLY OPENED

District 4 has officially launched the city's largest autonomous skyway drone corridor, featuring over 200 high-capacity automated landing pads. The new corridor operates at heights of 120 meters above ground level, isolating aerial cargo transport from pedestrian walkways.

Powered by the Quantum AI Core, delivery transit times across District 4 have dropped from 15 minutes to under 3 minutes, with zero emissions and zero acoustic noise pollution.
